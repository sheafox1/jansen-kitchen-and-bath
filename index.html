How It Works

From consultation to finished remodel

How a Jansen project typically runs.

1. Design consultation

A designer meets you at home and helps make the tough decisions.

2. Design and selections

Floor plan, cabinets, colors, tile and fixtures, with an imaging preview.

3. Construction

We tear out the old space and install new plumbing, cabinets, tile, granite and glass.

4. Walk-through

Our project managers keep you informed, and projects are backed by an installation warranty.
